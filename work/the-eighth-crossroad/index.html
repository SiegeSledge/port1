Overview

The Eighth Crossroad (also titled The Eighth Sacrifice) is an educational narrative game on technology ethics and crisis decisions. It was developed as a team project advised by Prof. Dennis Morgan at Teachers College, Columbia University, in two forms at once: a tabletop game and a Twine digital version.

Design

  • A core narrative and a three-chapter ethical structure shared by the tabletop and Twine versions.
  • Asymmetric information between the two players and a “read, communicate, decide, reflect” loop that drives collaboration.
  • A Past / Future two-player, two-track narrative, and a consolidation of fragmentary narrative into a full three-chapter structure.

My role

  • Playtest guidance and UX writing designed to lower cognitive load.
  • Twine rapid prototyping and implementation of the digital version.
  • Tracking narrative, art, music and code progress with product-management methods.
  • Contributions to programming, level design and UI design, using Figma and Twine.

Playtesting and iteration

I ran three playtest rounds with 48 participants in total and three rounds of iteration, tuning replayability, difficulty balance, session length and whether the learning goals were actually delivered.

Outcome

  • Published on itch.io, with 20+ downloads.
  • Won a school-level second prize and the “Most Popular Award”.
